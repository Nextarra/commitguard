## Recording preparation

Use 1440px desktop or 1280px with readable zoom, remove personal browser data, keep all credentials offscreen, and narrate at a measured pace. Use original visuals and no copyrighted music. Keep the fixture banner visible during fixture shots. Do not put an actual sandbox buyer password into the public source; provide judge setup through an authorized secure process.

The first-prize goal informs execution quality; it is not a prediction of judging results. The equally weighted judging areas are implementation, design, specific impact, innovation and presentation; technical implementation breaks the first tie.
